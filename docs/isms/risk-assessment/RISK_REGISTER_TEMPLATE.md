## Treatment Options
- **Accept**: Risk is within acceptable threshold
- **Mitigate**: Implement controls to reduce risk
- **Transfer**: Transfer risk to third party (insurance, outsourcing)
- **Avoid**: Eliminate the risk by removing the asset or process
